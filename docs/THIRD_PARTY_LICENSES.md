The twelve material maps total approximately 3.9 MB. Original download URLs, asset names, source and processed SHA-256 checksums, byte sizes and encoding settings are retained in [sources.json](../public/assets/materials/sources.json). Normal maps use WebP quality 95; albedo and roughness use 88. Textures remain 1024² and are served locally. These are generic material references, not photographs of the Golden Gate site.

No externally authored HDR environments, photo backdrops or 3D models are shipped. The sky is Three.js's analytical Sky; its environment map is generated locally. Water, fog, terrain elevations, city blocks, bridge geometry, traffic, vessels and the favicon are procedural assets. The MIT project licence does not replace ODbL, font or third-party asset terms.
